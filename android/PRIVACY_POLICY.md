## How to Host Privacy Policy

### Option 1: Add to Your Website

1. **Create file**: `privacy-policy.html` or `privacy-policy.html`
2. **Upload** to your website root: `https://naranpuraviharsevagroup.com/privacy-policy.html`
3. **Link** in Play Store Console

### Option 2: Create Separate Page

1. **Create page** on your website
2. **URL**: `https://naranpuraviharsevagroup.com/privacy-policy`
3. **Link** in Play Store Console

### Option 3: Use Privacy Policy Generator

- **FreePrivacyPolicy.com**
- **PrivacyPolicyGenerator.info**

## Add to Play Store

1. **Go to Play Console**
   - App content → Privacy policy

2. **Enter URL**
   - `https://naranpuraviharsevagroup.com/privacy-policy`

3. **Save**
